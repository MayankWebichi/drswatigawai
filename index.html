🎓 What medical qualifications and teaching credentials does Dr. Swati Gawai hold?

Dr. Swati Gawai holds MBBS, MS (OBGY), DGO, FMAS (Minimal Access Surgery), and FICOG. She is an Assistant Professor, MUHS RECOGNISED Undergraduate (MBBS) & Postgraduate (MS OBGY) Teacher and Examiner at Sion Hospital (LTMMC), Mumbai. She is also Trained in Robotic Assisted Surgery and Laparoscopy, with over 16 years of clinical experience.

🤰 What High-Risk Pregnancy services are available?

Dr. Swati Gawai specializes in managing high-risk pregnancies, pre-eclampsia, gestational diabetes, twin gestations, recurrent miscarriages, intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR), and emergency caesarean & normal deliveries.

🤖 What robotic and laparoscopic surgeries are conducted?

Specialized keyhole robotic & laparoscopic procedures include Total Laparoscopic Hysterectomy (TLH), Laparoscopic Myomectomy (Fibroid Removal), Ovarian Cystectomy, Endometriosis Ablation, and Diagnostic Hystero-Laparoscopy for infertility.
